# Introduction

Welcome to the ** Modern Cyber Threat Analysis and Intelligence** course!

Users, devices, and applications within your organization are connected across on-premises and cloud platforms in a modern, distributed environment. There are security controls in place: firewalls enforce access control policy, access is restricted, and vulnerabilities are scanned and patched. In theory, the environment appears to be well protected.
Yet incidents persist across the industry, and post-incident analysis shows a consistent pattern. Unknown vulnerabilities or obvious technical bypasses can compromise systems. But most often, access is granted through valid credentials and trusted workflows.
Rather than forcing their way in, modern attackers simply operate within the existing rules. This shift redefines the security challenge. Effective defense now requires a deep understanding of the adversaries, their motivations, and the high-success techniques they favor over traditional software exploits. Isolated events, such as an unusual login or a phishing email, may seem minor on their own, but you must leverage threat intelligence to correlate these signals and uncover the malicious intent hidden within these events.
!(/images/topics//Introduction.svg)
This training enables you to:
1. Explain how the motivations and constraints of various threat actors result in predictable attack patterns and specific organizational impacts.
2. Explain how various threat actors typically gain initial access using distinct attack vectors across endpoints, identity, networks, and cloud applications.
3. Explain how initial access evolves into a complete breach through credential compromise, lateral movement, privilege abuse, and resulting organizational impact.
4. Explain how cyber threat intelligence is produced, shared, and consumed to provide ongoing awareness of attacker behavior and emerging risks.
